About the lab

A translational omics laboratory focused on obesity and metabolic health.

The Unidad de Investigación en Obesidad integrates clinical, molecular and computational approaches to investigate obesity, metabolic dysfunction and response to therapeutic interventions. At this stage, the laboratory focuses on genomics and metabolomics as core analytical platforms.

Genomics

We analyze genetic variation and gene-level information to explore biological mechanisms associated with obesity and metabolic disease.

Metabolomics

We identify metabolic signatures linked to obesity, metabolic health, disease progression and response to clinical or nutritional interventions.

Reproducible research

We develop documented computational workflows, scripts and analysis templates to promote transparency, reuse and responsible biomedical data science.

Research areas

Core scientific lines

Our current work is centered on genomics, metabolomics, obesity, metabolism and precision health.

Research principles

Responsible and reproducible biomedical data science

We promote transparent computational workflows while protecting sensitive clinical and human-derived data.

Reproducibility

Clear documentation, version control, workflow organization and reusable analysis templates.

Responsible data use

Restricted clinical or human-derived datasets are not publicly shared unless appropriate approvals allow it.
